ytenrich lays every YouTube page out as tiling panes and gives you your own recommendation lists, ranked by what you actually watch. To do that, it keeps a record of the recommendations YouTube shows you and the videos you open. The record stays in your browser.

Tiling layout
• The player, details, chapters, comments, chat and lists sit side by side in resizable panes with tabs, each scrolling on its own. Other pages sit next to YouTube's guide.
• Drag a tab to split, join or reorder panes.
• Nine workspaces for each kind of page (watch, home, channel and the rest). Switch with the numbers next to the YouTube logo, or with Alt+Shift+1 to 9.
• Shorts play in the regular player, and video descriptions open by themselves.
• YouTube Music too: the page you browse, the player and Up next sit side by side, so you can keep browsing while a video plays.

Recommendations on your terms
• Your own lists of Videos, Shorts, Mix lists and Channels, gathered from everything YouTube recommends to you, including the lists your layout hides.
• Ranked by the channels and topics you open or pass over, and kept varied, so no single channel or topic takes over.
• Each list has a search box and its own settings: what it shows, all channels or only the one you are watching, ranked or newest first, grouped by channel.
• A "Not interested" button on every YouTube recommendation: one click, without opening the menu.
• Optional: hide or dim the videos YouTube keeps showing you and you never open.

Statistics
• See which videos YouTube keeps showing you, how often, and which of them you opened, day by day.

Privacy
• No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Nothing is sent to the developer.
• Turn the record off or clear it at any time in Settings.
• The Chrome and Edge versions load thumbnails from YouTube's image servers (you can turn this off). The Firefox version loads none.
• Open source (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/lkjsxc/ytenrich
